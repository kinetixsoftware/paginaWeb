What is the difference between a ticket and a service request?
Use a ticket to report a problem or ask for general help. Create a service request from an equipment page when you need maintenance, repair, inspection, or installation.
How do I create a request for equipment?
Sign in, open Inventory, select the equipment assigned to you, and choose the service request option. Select the service type and priority, then clearly describe the issue or work needed.
What information should I include in the description?
Explain what is happening, when it started, who is affected, and what you have already tried. Include any error message, but never include passwords or other confidential information.
What do the priority levels mean?
Low is for work that can be scheduled; Medium means the work is needed within a reasonable timeframe; High is for urgent issues that interrupt important work or affect multiple people. Choose the level that best reflects the impact.
Who can see and work on my request?
A request is automatically associated with the location registered on your account. Technicians at that location can view and manage it, and you can view your own requests from the Requests tab. Contact an administrator if your location is incorrect.
What do the request statuses mean?
“Created” means it was submitted; “Reviewed” means the technical team is evaluating or working on it; “Solved” means a solution was recorded; and “Closed” means the process is complete.
How can I check the progress of my request?
Open Tickets and select the Requests tab. Choose a request to see its status, creation date, and action history.
What does a technician do when a request comes in?
The technician can review the details, update the status, and record actions in the history. After completing the work, they mark the request as solved and can then close it.
What if the equipment is missing or is not assigned to my account?
Make sure you are signed in with your institutional account and check whether the equipment is assigned to you in Inventory. If it is still missing, create a ticket with its name or asset code and the relevant location.
How do I report an urgent issue or one affecting multiple people?
Create a ticket or service request with High priority. Describe the impact and include the location and affected people or teams. Priority helps the team organize work but does not replace the institution’s emergency channels.
